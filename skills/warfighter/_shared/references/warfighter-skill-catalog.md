# Warfighter Skill Catalog

This catalog maps warfighter skill coverage across major military action domains.

## Core Domain Coverage

- Mission command and decision support
- Intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, and targeting
- Land, air, maritime, littoral, riverine, and subterranean operations
- Space, cyber, and electromagnetic operations
- Fires, protection, sustainment, force generation, and reconstitution
- Civil-military operations, coalition integration, and strategic competition

## Suggested Cross-Skill Pairings

- Campaign planning + ISR tasking + targeting + battle damage assessment
- Counter-UAS + air and missile defense + base defense + deception planning
- Contested logistics + mobility deployment + maintenance + operational energy
- Cyber mission planning + zero trust hardening + CEMA integration
- Space domain awareness + SATCOM defense + missile warning integration

## Minimum Product Quality Standard

Every skill output should include:

- Recommendation with at least one alternative
- Decision points with timing
- Staff action tracker (owner and suspense)
- Sources and protocol details
- Confidence, gaps, and branch triggers
