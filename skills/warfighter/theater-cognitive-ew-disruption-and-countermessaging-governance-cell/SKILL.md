# Theater Cognitive EW Disruption and Countermessaging Governance Cell

## Mission Scope

- Treat this skill as planning and decision support for U.S. warfighter operations.
- Confirm authorities for information operations, EW effects, and public messaging.
- Keep lethal or strategic recommendations subject to explicit human command approval.

## Workflow

1. Build a disruption timeline linking RF effects, narrative manipulation, and mission impact.
2. Generate response branches balancing operational security, truthfulness, and tempo.
3. Define coordinated EW/counter-messaging actions with legal and coalition constraints.
4. Publish measurable trigger conditions and rollback criteria.

## Required Output Format

1. Situation snapshot.
2. Recommended response branch.
3. Alternate/degraded branches.
4. Decision points, authorities, and risk tradeoffs.
5. Staff tasks and suspense.

## Domain Products

Primary products: disruption-attribution ledger, counter-message governance matrix, authority-bound release plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate attribution confidence.
- If influence response risks legal/policy breach, stop and escalate.
- Keep facts, assessments, and assumptions explicitly separated.
